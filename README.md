**Quick Park** is a parking management platform that connects drivers with parking space owners. I built this project to make finding parking spaces easier and help parking owners manage and promote their available spaces through one platform.


## Problem I Wanted to Solve

Finding a parking space in busy areas can be time consuming. Drivers often struggle to find suitable parking locations and get information about pricing. On the other hand, many parking space owners don't have an easy way to promote or manage their available spaces.

I wanted to solve these problems by bringing both drivers and parking owners together on one platform.

## My Solution

With Quick Park, drivers can explore parking locations, check details and pricing, and make online payments. Parking owners can apply to become partners and manage their parking spaces. Admins can manage users and review partner applications.

## Key Features

- **For Drivers:** Explore parking locations, view details, check hourly/daily pricing, and make online payments.
- **For Partners:** Apply as a parking partner, submit parking information, and track application status.
- **For Admins:** Manage users, control user roles, and approve or reject partner applications.
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication with Google Sign-In and protected routes.
- **Payment:** Stripe integration with payment verification.
- **Parking Search:** Location based parking discovery with map integration.
